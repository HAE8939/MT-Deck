# Prompt

Deconstruct this piece of content into its transferable mechanisms:

1. Hook — what stops the scroll (visual / language / emotion / cognition / result)?
2. Problem — what tension does it open?
3. Development — how does it hold attention beat by beat?
4. Payoff — what resolves the tension?
5. Emotion — what feeling does it leave behind?
6. CTA — what action or continuation does it invite?

For each mechanism, state whether it is tied to the specific topic or transferable. End with a one-paragraph reusable structure with the topic removed.

# Notes

Reconstruction ≠ copying. Keep the mechanism, replace the topic, scene, case, and expression.
