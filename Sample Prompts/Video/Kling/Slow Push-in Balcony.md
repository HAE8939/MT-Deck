# Prompt

Slow dolly push-in toward the balcony. The frame opens on a quiet morning balcony: linen curtains moving gently in the breeze, a cup of tea on the small round table, soft morning light crawling across the floor. The camera moves forward at a constant slow speed, no shake, no cut. Objects stay in place, proportions stay true. Light gradually warms as the camera approaches. Nothing else moves.

# Notes

DIRECT VIDEO pattern: no character, the light change is the only narrative.
